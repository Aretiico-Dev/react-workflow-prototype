# Brand And Voice

## Brand Character

- Tone: dependable, regulated, professional, and enterprise-oriented.
- Domain: trust services, certificates, identity verification, organisation verification, access control, and audit-friendly workflows.
- Emotional register: calm and competent rather than warm, playful, or aspirational.
- Visual stance: formal portal UI with restrained branding accents.

## Logo And Brand Assets

- Use the Aretiico wordmark/logo as a compact brand anchor, not a large hero element.
- Keep logo usage functional: auth card, sidebar header, dashboard card header, or compact app-shell branding.
- Do not turn the brand into a consumer-tech aesthetic.

## Copy Style

- Use direct task-oriented labels such as “Certificates”, “Organisations”, “Verifications”, “Profile”, “Tasks”, and “Invites”.
- Prefer instructional and compliance-aware copy over promotional copy.
- Use clear status language: pending, verified, revoked, issued, expired, rejected, approved.
- Keep body copy concise and specific. The app usually explains workflows, next steps, or current status.

## Product Context

- Current business framing: Allow customers to purchase and manage certificates, verifications, and related trust workflows.
- Typical user jobs: register and sign in; verify identity, domains, email, and organisations; request, approve, download, revoke, and reorder certificates; manage tasks, invites, and profile settings

## Do This

- Write UI text like an enterprise workflow product for certificate and identity management.
- Keep screens grounded in trust, verification, auditability, and operational clarity.
- Avoid lifestyle copy, playful mascots, hype phrases, or consumer-fintech language.
