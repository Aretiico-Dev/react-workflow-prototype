# Typography

## Font Family

- Primary UI font: `'Titillium Web', sans-serif`
- Heading font: `'Titillium Web', sans-serif`
- Buttons use the same family with weight 500 and uppercase treatment.

## Typography Notes

- Headings are assertive but not oversized. The portal relies on structure and density rather than dramatic type.
- Body copy is compact and practical, generally around 0.875rem for primary body text.
- Buttons and overlines use uppercase treatment in the theme scale.

## Do This

- Use Titillium Web consistently for headings, body, buttons, labels, cards, navigation, and table-adjacent text.
- Keep headings compact and portal-like rather than editorial.
- Avoid introducing elegant serif, geometric sans, or consumer-product typography.
